# The digest

One Slack post a week. A section with nothing true to say is omitted, never filled.

```text
:ear: *What the market said this week*
_<the theme across the picks, one line | "Quiet week: nothing worth joining">_

*Worth joining*
• <author>, <author title>: <one-line gist> — <why, citing the ICP or pain line> (<post url>) · <N> comments

*Warm engagers*
• <name>, <headline as written> on <author>'s post: "<first sentence of their comment>" (<profile url>)

_Searched: <searchKeywords as written> · <N> posts read, <M> already surfaced_
```

## Rules

- **Worth joining**: at most five, ranked by fit then by conversation. Each cites the context line
  that decided it. A post that cannot be tied to the context is not picked.
- **Warm engagers**: commenters only, at most three per post and ten in all, whose headline matches an
  ICP persona. The employer is what the headline states, or nothing. The quote is theirs, verbatim.
- **Comments not read**: a pick above the comment cap says so on its line instead of listing engagers.
- **Searched**: always present, quiet weeks included, so silence never reads as a broken run.
