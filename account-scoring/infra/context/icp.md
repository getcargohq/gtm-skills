We sell to B2B SaaS companies between 50 and 1,000 employees running an
outbound or hybrid GTM motion. The economic buyer is the Head of Growth, VP
Sales, or RevOps lead. Strong fits already use a sales engagement tool and a
CRM, and feel pain around data quality, routing, and manual enrichment.

Disqualifiers: B2C, agencies, fewer than 10 employees, no sales team.
