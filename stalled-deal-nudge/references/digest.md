# The digest

One Slack post per owner per week, deals ordered by amount, largest first. A line with nothing true
to say is omitted, never filled.

```text
:hourglass: *Stalled deals · <owner name> · <ISO week>*
_<count> open deals with no logged activity in <QUIET_DAYS>+ days._

*<deal name>* — <stage>, <amount>, closes <close date> · quiet <N> days, flagged <K> Mondays running
Last: <date> "<quoted line from the last note, meeting or call>"
Why now: <one sentence>
Draft: > <at most four sentences, for the owner to send>
<HubSpot deal link>
```

## Rules for each line

- **Flagged K Mondays running** appears only when the ledger has earlier rows for the deal.
- **Last** is a quote, with its date. A deal with no logged activity says so instead.
- **Why now** is specific to the deal: a close date about to slip, a next step nobody took, an
  objection left unanswered. "It has been a while" is not a reason.
- **Draft** picks up from the quoted line. No bracketed placeholders; if a fact is missing, the
  draft is written around it. It is never sent by the agent.

An owner with no stalled deals gets no post. A Monday when nobody has one posts nothing.
