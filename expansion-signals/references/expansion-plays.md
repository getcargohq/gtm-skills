# Expansion plays (example for your context)

The analyst reads what counts as an expansion moment, and which play answers it, from the workspace
context. Copy this file to `context/expansion-plays.md` in your project and rewrite it for what you
sell. It is an example written for a fictional seller of data-pipeline monitoring.

## What we sell into an existing customer

- **More seats**: priced per monitored pipeline owner. Grows with the data team.
- **The governance add-on**: lineage and audit, bought once a compliance owner exists.
- **Annual prepay**: a discount for moving from monthly to annual.

## Signals and the play that answers each

| Signal            | What it looks like                                                            | Play                                                             |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `expansion`       | Hiring three or more data engineers; a new data leader; a new product line    | Seat expansion call with usage numbers                           |
| `expansion`       | A funding round in the last 120 days                                          | Annual prepay offer before the budget is planned                 |
| `expansion`       | A compliance or audit hire; a public SOC 2 or ISO announcement                | Governance add-on demo                                           |
| `repeat_purchase` | Two or more orders at a regular interval, the next one due                    | Renewal call booked before the interval ends, at the last price  |
| `renewal`         | The anniversary is the only reason                                            | Standard renewal, owned by the account manager                   |
| `at_risk`         | Layoffs in the data team; a new leader from a competitor's customer           | Executive check-in before the renewal conversation               |

## What never counts

- A press mention with no date.
- A job post older than 120 days.
- Anything about a different company with a similar name.
