# The inbound note

One Slack post per researched contact, to the fixed channel.

```text
:inbox_tray: *<first> <last>*, <job title> at <company> — tier *<A | B | C | disqualified>*
<the brief: at most three sentences>
_<the rationale: two sentences naming the rubric lines that decided the tier>_
Owner: <@slack-user | HubSpot owner <id> | No owner yet> · Source: <hs_analytics_source>
Sources: <every page the brief or the tier relied on>
```

## Rules

- **The brief** says who they are, what the company does, and why they might be talking to us now.
  Every fact in it is in the CRM record or on a listed source page.
- **The rationale** names the rubric lines. "Good fit" is not a rationale.
- **Owner** is a Slack mention only when the owner map has the HubSpot owner. The note never
  suggests or changes an owner.
- **Disqualified** leads are posted too, with the reason (student, vendor, competitor, job seeker),
  so a wrong disqualification is visible. Drop them from the channel with the `a-and-b-only`
  variation, not by editing the agent.
